# Focus Blocking

Terms for the personal system that coordinates study sessions across Android and Chrome.

## Language

**Allowed apps list**:
A reusable selection of Android apps that remain available during a study session. Apps outside the selection are blocked. A list can be selected before a session starts and cannot change during that session.
_Avoid_: Access profile, phone blocklist

**Essentials list**:
The fixed selection of essential phone functions used when no custom allowed apps list is selected. It cannot be edited or deleted.

**Desktop site blocklist**:
The shared selection of websites that Chrome blocks during an active study session. Sites can be added during a session; existing selections remain locked until it ends.

**Study session**:
A timed period started on Android or Chrome that records study time. Android keeps the selected allowed apps available, while Chrome blocks selected desktop sites until the session ends.
_Avoid_: Planned block, focus block

**Session review**:
The required record after a study session ends. The user records the task and outcome, and can add notes or remaining work. Another session can start after the review is complete.

**Automatic break**:
An optional rest period after a session review. Phone restrictions are off during the break, which the user can end early.

**Daily review**:
A summary of recorded study sessions, study time, focus areas, and progress for one day. Recorded time is compared with the daily study target.

**Weekly review**:
A Monday-to-Sunday summary of recorded study time by focus area and progress.

**Focus area**:
A user-defined subject or project to which a study session contributes its recorded time.

**Study-hours target**:
The configurable daily amount of recorded study time used for comparison in reviews. Missing the target does not restrict phone or desktop use.

**Activity record**:
A completed activity recorded without a study session. Its duration counts toward the daily study target only when explicitly marked as study.

**Unrecorded interval**:
A period of the day for which no study session or activity record is available.

**Rest day**:
A chosen weekday without an expected study target. The user can still study voluntarily.

**Exceptional day**:
A day marked with a reason, such as illness or travel, so its review does not present an unmet target as a failure.

**Offline enforcement**:
Continued app or site blocking on a disconnected device until the end of a study session it already knows about. Devices reconcile session changes when connectivity returns.

**Study reminder**:
A Chrome notification at 21:00 that repeats every 30 minutes while no study session has started that day. It invites the user to begin a session and does not restrict browsing.

**Device**:
An installation of the Android app or Chrome extension that participates in the user's study sessions.
